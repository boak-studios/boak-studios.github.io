Privacy policy

Effective 12 November 2026. This policy covers Boak Studios Analytics, the studio's internal tool for reading YouTube statistics of the studio's own channels.

Who uses the app

Only Boak Studios, signed in with the Google account that manages the studio's channels. The app is not offered to the public and no one else signs in to it.

What data the app accesses

With your permission through Google's sign-in, the app requests read-only access (yt-analytics.readonly and youtube.readonly) to the analytics of the signed-in account's own YouTube channel: per-video views, engaged views, watch time, average percentage viewed, audience retention, subscribers gained, likes, shares and comment counts. It does not read private messages, personal profile details of viewers, or any data of other channels, and it cannot upload, change or delete anything.

How the data is used and stored

The statistics are saved as files on the studio's own computer and used only to evaluate and improve the studio's videos. The sign-in token is stored on that computer with access restricted to the studio's user account.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer the data to anyone. No advertising, no third-party analytics, no cookies on this website.

Google API Services

Boak Studios Analytics' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. Revoking it stops all reads; stored statistics are deleted on request.
